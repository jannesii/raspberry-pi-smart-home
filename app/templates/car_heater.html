        Logs & maintenance

        Weather collection

        Station observations are archived every ten minutes and the parking-point forecast is checked every thirty minutes, with or without heating. Station history is backfilled 24 hours on first start, and gaps up to seven days old are repaired after an outage; backfilled values keep their real retrieval time. Only forecast versions actually received are kept; forecasts never control heating. Values a source did not report are source limitations, not collector failures.

          Export archived weather

          Observations are selected by source time and include every revision; forecasts by valid time and include every version received; coverage lists collected stretches, gaps and unchecked forecast stretches. Exports keep source text, units, received missing values and backfill labels. Original replies remain separate downloads.

          Restart device?

          The device will reboot and communication may be interrupted. A restart acknowledgement does not prove the relay is OFF. Use Stop heating in Heating to request OFF.
