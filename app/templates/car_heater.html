Weather collection

Station observations are archived every ten minutes and the parking-point forecast is checked every thirty minutes, with or without heating. Station history is backfilled 24 hours on first start, and gaps up to seven days old are repaired after an outage; backfilled values keep their real retrieval time. Only forecast versions actually received are kept; forecasts never control heating. Values a source did not report are source limitations, not collector failures.
